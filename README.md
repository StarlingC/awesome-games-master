## World building

### Campaign management
* [Chronica](https://chronica.ventures/features) - :money_with_wings: A campaign planner and manager with loads of world building tools. The online campaign manager has system flexibility.
* [Chronicler](https://chronicler.pro/) - [:unlock:](https://github.com/mak-kirkland/chronicler) :computer: [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/c/ChroniclerPro) A versatile, cross-platform worldbuilding app that can be used as a simple note-taking app, a private local wiki, a writing tool for a novel, or a TTRPG campaign manager.
* [ChronoGrapher](https://chronographer.net/info) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/ChronoGrapher) :money_with_wings: ChronoGrapher is an extremely flexible worldbuilding tool for writers, worldbuilders and game masters.
* [Easy Almanac](https://easyalmanac.com/) - :robot: :money_with_wings: A campaign manager that keeps one shared record for the whole table: linked sessions, NPCs and places, player notes the GM approves, and flags when notes contradict each other.
* [Fantasia Archive](https://fantasiaarchive.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/c/vishiri) :computer: [:unlock:](https://github.com/Elvanos/fantasia-archive) Free offline worldbuilding and story creation tool.
* [Fantasy calendar](https://app.fantasy-calendar.com/) - :money_with_wings: [:unlock:](https://github.com/fantasycalendar/Fantasy-Calendar) Create a detailed calendar and track time with a nice interface.
* [GMBinder](https://www.gmbinder.com/) - :money_with_wings: Create homebrew content and browse other creations in a professional format.
* [Kanka](https://kanka.io/) - :money_with_wings: Kanka is an easy and flexible tool to worldbuild and manage your RPG campaign online. Create and organize people, places, organizations, events and much more.
* [Lore Forge](https://loreforge.com/) - :money_with_wings: :computer: An app for writers & worldbuilders. Organize your thoughts and cross-reference anything at any time.
* [Lorevia](https://lorevia.gg/) - :money_with_wings: :robot: Lorevia builds a campaign wiki from the session notes a table already writes. Users paste notes in any state of roughness, and it drafts pages for the NPCs, locations, quests, items, and factions it finds, plus a "story so far" recap.
* [Mordex](https://mordex.world/) - :money_with_wings: A free, browser-based all-in-one campaign manager: a connected world of NPCs, factions, locations and lore, plus a built-in VTT, combat tracker, session prep and shareable recaps.
* [Obsidian Portal](https://www.obsidianportal.com/) - :money_with_wings: Obsidian Portal takes the pain out of managing your tabletop RPG.
* [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) - :computer: A powerful knowledge base that operates on a local folder of plain text Markdown files.
* [Realm Works](https://www.wolflair.com/realmworks/) - :money_with_wings: Realm Works is a database software and worldbuilding tool for roleplaying games.
* [Realmweaver](https://realmweaver.io/) - :money_with_wings: :robot: An AI creative director that helps writers, game masters, and world-builders develop deep, consistent worlds through guided sessions, all organized into a living wiki.
* [Scabard](https://www.scabard.com/pbs/) - :money_with_wings: Scabard is a rules neutral RPG campaign manager and planner. They have campaigns for D&D (various versions), Pathfinder, Traveller, Savage Worlds, Warhammer, and a host of others.
* [ScryRPG](https://scryrpg.com/) - :robot: :money_with_wings: A campaign manager for D&D and other tabletop RPGs with shared party inventory, character sheets, shops, loot, a linked lore wiki, and an AI familiar.
* [Six Axes](https://www.six-axes.com/) - :robot: Six Axes turns a recorded tabletop session into the record most groups never keep. It captures the game by voice, transcribes it, and generates a session recap, an automated campaign wiki (places, NPCs, lore, and factions), and a log of every roll.
* [The Goblin's Notebook](https://www.the-goblin.net/) - [<img src="/images/kofi.png" height="14" alt="Kofi">](https://ko-fi.com/goblinsnotebook) A single-pane view of your tabletop RPG campaign notes for pre-session planning, in-session note taking and post-session recapping.
* [Trails Weaver](https://www.trailsweaver.com/) - :money_with_wings: Visual worldbuilding app and wiki for game masters and writers, with an infinite canvas, interactive maps, family trees, and a relationship graph.
* [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/) - :money_with_wings: A tool for managing your world and keeping track of things in your campaign.

### Generators
#### Generating maps
* [Azgaar's Fantasy map generator](https://azgaar.github.io/Fantasy-Map-Generator/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/azgaar) :game_die: [:unlock:](https://github.com/Azgaar/Fantasy-Map-Generator) Open source world map generator with a lot of customization.
* [Watabou](https://watabou.itch.io) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/watawatabou) Home of many procedural generators.
  * [Medieval fantasy city generator](https://watabou.itch.io/medieval-fantasy-city-generator) - :game_die: City generator with several customization options.
  * [Village generator](https://watabou.itch.io/village-generator) - :game_die: Simple village generator.

#### Generating shops / towns / characters
* [5e magic shop generator](https://5emagic.shop/generate) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/5emagic) :game_die: :dragon: Generates stock for magic shops with opinionated pricing. Also includes other tools such as travel time calculator and teleportation circle generator.
* [DnD 5e / Coriolis store generator](http://dndstores.azurewebsites.net/index.html) - :game_die: Generates the stock and prices of various stores based on city size.
* [donjon random generators](http://donjon.bin.sh/) - :game_die: Generators for all sorts including landmass, names, dungeons, calendars, shops, etc.
* [Kenji's Game Master Tools](https://cros.land/kenjis-dungeon-master-tools/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/c/ai_rpg_tookit) :robot: :money_with_wings: :game_die: :dragon: AI-powered generators for statblocks, encounters, dungeons, NPCs, magic items and settings, plus chase, loot and inventory tools. Free to use, with daily limits on generation.
* [Town generator](https://eigengrausgenerator.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/join/eigengrausgenerator) :game_die: [:unlock:](https://github.com/ryceg/Eigengrau-s-Essential-Establishment-Generator/) A generator for town buildings and characters with narration.

#### Name generators
* [Fantasy Name Generators](https://www.fantasynamegenerators.com/) - Names for all your fantasy characters.
* [NamesPlease](https://namesplease.app/) - :game_die: Name generators for towns, cities, kingdoms, taverns, ships, rivers, mountains and swords, plus character names. Runs in the browser, no sign-up.
* [Party group name generator](https://www.chaosgen.com/fantasy/party-name) - Because sometimes it's hard coming up with a good name.
* [Random book generator](https://www.dndspeak.com/2019/06/11/random-book-generator/) - A random book title and synopsis.

#### NPC generators
* [Character generator](https://tetra-cube.com/dnd/dnd-char-gen.html) - :game_die: :dragon: Generates random DnD 5e characters from the options in the sourcebooks with character art.
* [Statblock builder](https://tetra-cube.com/dnd/dnd-statblock.html) - :dragon: A DnD custom monster stat block generator in the style of the source books.

#### Misc generators
* [Ancient quests](http://ancientquests.com/) - Short written descriptions for a number of things: worlds, rooms, paintings, factions, history, items, effects, etc.
* [Constellation generator](https://www.darkestofnights.com/gen_cons.php) - Generates constellation with image and star names.
* [Holiday generator](http://chaoticshiny.com/holidaygen.php) - A description of a certain holiday including the time of year and historical significance.
* [Job board generator](https://www.ionzone.com/rpg/random-job-posting.php) - A graphical job board with fun little quest ideas.
* [Law generator](https://www.rangen.co.uk/world/lawgen.php) - A random list of potentially legal and illegal things along with punishments.
* [Not another tavern generator](https://thecoppersanctum.github.io/thecoppersanctum/taverns.html) - More detailed tavern with some settings to tweak.
* [Spell book generator](https://5emagic.shop/spellbook/generate) - Generate a random level appropriate spell book.
* [Tavern generator](https://www.herebetaverns.com/tavern-generator) - Random taverns with floorplan, menu, and staff.
* [Trap generator](https://www.5thdnd.com/dungeon-trap) - Imaginative and clever trap ideas.
* [Perchance](https://perchance.org/welcome) - Create your own random generators.
* [Chartopia](https://chartopia.d12dev.com/) - A website where you can create, share, and find random tables and generators for role-playing games.

## Session prep

### Battle / encounter maps
* [AutoREALM](https://sourceforge.net/projects/autorealm/) - :unlock: AutoREALM is a Free GNU mapping software (a "cartographer") that can design maps of castles, cities, dungeons and more.
* [Dungeon generator](https://dungen.app/dungen/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/DungeonChannel) :game_die: Generates random dungeons with some customization.
* [Dungeon scrawl](https://dungeonscrawl.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/bePatron?u=35362025) Easy to use tool for building simple maps.
* [DungeonFog](https://www.dungeonfog.com/) - :money_with_wings: An online encounter map builder and partial vtt.
* [Dungeondraft](https://dungeondraft.net/) - :moneybag: :computer: A dungeon / encounter builder with customisable assets.
* [MapForge](https://www.mapforge-software.com) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/heruca) :moneybag: :computer: Software for creating dungeons with lots of add-ons and other assets.
* [Textures](https://www.textures.com/) - :money_with_wings: A library of thousands of textures that can be used for terrain.
* [Tiled Map Editor](https://www.mapeditor.org/) - [:unlock:](https://github.com/mapeditor/tiled) [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/join/bjorn?) :computer: Tiled is a general purpose tile map editor. It's built to be easy to use, yet flexible enough to work with varying game engines, whether your game is an RPG, platformer or Breakout clone.
* [Token maker](https://rolladvantage.com/tokenstamp/) - A simple tool for quickly generating tokens from images.

### Handout helpers

* [Cartography Assets](http://cartographyassets.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/cartographyassets) A community driven hub of maps and map making assets.
* [Hextml](http://hextml.playest.net/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/playest) A hex based relief map/dungeon maker with collaborative tools.
* [Inkarnate](https://inkarnate.com/) - :money_with_wings: A world map maker with a large library of assets and many features.
* [RPG Map Editor](https://www.rpgmapeditor.com/) - :money_with_wings: :moneybag: :computer: RPG Map Editor is a browser-based battle map maker for Dungeon Masters and TTRPG players.
* [Wonderdraft](https://www.wonderdraft.net/) - :moneybag: :computer: A world map maker with customisable assets.
* [Worldographer](https://worldographer.com/) - :moneybag: :computer: An integrated overland hex mapper for worlds or regions; settlement vector mapper for cities or villages; and square battle mapper for dungeons.

#### Items handouts
* [Fantasy card generator](https://crobi.github.io/rpg-cards) - [:unlock:](https://github.com/crobi/rpg-cards) A tool for generating printable cards in easy to read formats for spells, monster stats, etc.

#### Misc handouts
* [Coat of Arms Maker](https://coamaker.com/) - :money_with_wings: Coat of Arms Maker is a freemium drag & drop generator for creating fantasy artwork like family crests, medieval banners, coins, and tokens.

## Running the game

### VTTs
* [Alchemy VTTRPG](https://alchemyrpg.com/) - :money_with_wings: Alchemy is a Virtual Tabletop (VTT) with a focus on cinematic elements, like music and environmental backgrounds, motion overlays, and more.
* [ArcForge](https://www.myarcforge.com/) - :money_with_wings: :robot: ArcForge is a GM workspace built for tabletop RPG game masters who want to run better sessions without losing immersion. Instead of switching between a dozen browser tabs mid-session, everything you need is in one place.
* [Astral Tabletop](https://www.astraltabletop.com/) - :money_with_wings: An online platform with built in maps and assets.
* [Battlegrounds: RPG Edition](https://battlegroundsgames.com/battlegrounds-rpg-edition/) - :moneybag: :computer: Battlegrounds: RPG Edition (aka BRPG) is virtual tabletop software designed to enhance your pen-and-paper roleplaying games, whether your players are in the same room, or on the other side of the world.
* [Calamus](https://trapstreetstudios.com/theCalamus) - :money_with_wings: :computer: A map-making companion for tabletop RPGs, like Dungeons & Dragons or Pathfinder.
* [Cauldron VTT](https://www.cauldron-vtt.net/) - [:unlock:](https://gitlab.com/hsleisink/cauldron/-/raw/master/LICENSE) Cauldron VTT is a web application to run tabletop role playing games, like Dungeons & Dragons and Pathfinder, online.
* [Dungeoneer](https://www.ogreforge.me/Dungeoneer/) - [:unlock:](https://www.github.com/durtur/dungeoneer) :computer: A VTT with DM tools for 5th edition D&D. Includes an initiative tracker, random table support, challenge rating calculators and more.
* [Embers: Tabletop RPG Toolkit](https://app.embersds.com/) - :money_with_wings: Embers is a toolkit for TTRPG players and game masters. It includes three tools and a virtual tabletop without extra subscription. It's free for your main characters, campaign, and world with no ads.
* [Epic World Builder](https://epicworldbuilder.com/) - :money_with_wings: EpicWorldBuilder.com gives you a unified space to build worlds, design maps, run campaigns, and play online with friends without juggling multiple apps.
* [EpicTable](http://www.epictable.com/) - :moneybag: :computer: EpicTable is a virtual tabletop that allows you and your friends to play your favorite pen-and-paper roleplaying games, regardless of whether you’re sitting across the table or across the world from one another.
* [Fantasy Grounds](http://www.fantasygrounds.com/home/home.php) - :moneybag: :computer: A role playing platform with support for many systems.
* [FoundryVTT](https://foundryvtt.com/) - :moneybag: A modern role playing platform with a large community and developer base and integrates with many other tools.
* [GM Forge](https://store.steampowered.com/app/842250/GM_Forge__Virtual_Tabletop/) - :moneybag: :computer: GM Forge is a Standalone Virtual Tabletop and is a sandbox for GMs. Friends can join through web browsers for free once the GM purchases a copy.
* [MapTool](https://www.rptools.net/toolbox/maptool/) - :computer: [:unlock:](https://github.com/RPTools/maptool) An advanced open source tool for running games with advanced vision blocking and macro support.
> * [Mythic table](https://www.mythictable.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/mythictable) :computer: [:unlock:](https://gitlab.com/mythicteam/mythictable/) Still in development open source platform.
* [Narrantium](https://narrantium.com/) - :robot: :money_with_wings: Narrative-first tabletop RPG engine for Game Masters to build branching adventures and run them in live multiplayer sessions.
* [Nexoria](https://nexoria.chat/) - :computer: :money_with_wings: A virtual tabletop for D&D and Pathfinder with chat, voice and video, and other features built in, so your group schedules, plays, and talks in one place.
* [One More Multiverse](https://www.playmultiverse.com/) - :money_with_wings: [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/OneMoreMultiverse) One More Multiverse (OMM) is a place to play tabletop roleplaying games like the 5e SRD, Blades in the Dark, Yazeba's Bed and Breakfast.
* [Owlbear Rodeo](https://www.owlbear.rodeo/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/owlbearrodeo) A very simple browser based platform that is easy to pick up and requires minimal setup.
* [Quest Portal](https://www.questportal.com/) - :robot: :money_with_wings: Quest Portal is a powerful virtual tabletop (VTT) for playing TTRPGs on desktop and mobile devices. Quest Portal offers a seamless blend of innovative features, allowing you to dive into thrilling adventures with friends.
* [Roll20](https://roll20.net/) - :money_with_wings: A popular role playing platform with lots of documentation.
* [Tableplop](https://new.tableplop.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/tableplop/) Tableplop is an easy to learn, fun to use virtual tabletop for role playing games. It includes everything you need to play TTRPGs remotely apart from audio chat and works great on mobiles and computers.
* [Tabletop Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/286160/Tabletop_Simulator/) - :moneybag: :computer: A physics sandbox for tabletop games, supporting 3D environments and custom mods.
* [Talespire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/720620/TaleSpire/) - :moneybag: :computer: TaleSpire is a beautiful way to play pen and paper RPGs online. Bring your stories to life and embark upon campaigns together with your friends, regardless of where you are in the world.

### Chat
* [Dungeons with Friends](https://dwf.styrognome.com/) - :moneybag: :computer: Dungeons with friends lets you easily set up, DM, and play tabletop roleplaying games from anywhere on your mobile phone. This virtual table top is designed for asynchronous play - so you don't have to find a time that works for all of your players.
* [RoleGate](https://www.rolegate.com/) - :money_with_wings: Role Gate is a platform for playing tabletop RPGs through chat, allowing flexible participation at your own pace, with features like separated in-character/out-of-character chat, character sheet editor, interactive helpers, and party splitting.

### Audio
* [Audio Forge](https://www.slashpaf.com/audioforge/) - :money_with_wings: :moneybag: :computer: RPG soundboard and music player for D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu and more. Import your own files, layer music with ambience, trigger one-shots, and run everything offline.
* [Bardica](https://bardica.com/) - :money_with_wings: Easy to use soundboard. It doesn't matter if it is Dungeons & Dragons, Pathfinder or any other roleplaying game you are playing, background music or custom sound effects will help you reach the next level.
* [Bardic Tools](https://bardic.tools/) - :money_with_wings: Bardic Tools is an intuitive, easy-to-use soundboard for TTRPGs. Because great sound should be a cantrip, not a ritual spell.
* [Fantasy Fader](https://fantasyfader.com/) - :moneybag: :money_with_wings: An audio management tool for tabletop game masters. Fantasy Fader allows game masters to combine music and ambiance from YouTube or their personal library and adjust volume, fade between tracks, make custom soundboards, and much more.
* [Onion Board](https://onion-alien.github.io/onion-board/) - :computer: [:unlock:](https://github.com/Onion-Alien/onion-board) A free Windows soundboard for running games over Discord: pads with hotkeys play music, ambience and effects straight into your mic, with loops, per-sound volume and fades, and a phone remote so you can trigger sounds away from the keyboard.
* [Pocket Bard](https://www.pocketbard.app/) - :computer: :money_with_wings: Pocket Bard is a fully immersive audio experience for your tabletop RPG game session. With just a tap, change your entire soundscape to match the tone of your session - from moment to moment.
* [RPG Sounds](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1480140/RPG_Sounds/) - :computer: :moneybag: RPG Sounds is an audio tool for Dungeon Masters and TTRPG Players.
* [Syrinscape](https://syrinscape.com/) - :moneybag: :money_with_wings: :computer: A sound system application that allows dynamic layering of different sounds.
* [Tabletop Audio](https://tabletopaudio.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/tabletopaudio) A library of 10 minute soundtracks for different ambiences and sound effects.

### Encounter planning / running

Subscription-free and designed to be used on all devices online. Currently offering a battle map and character sheets for Pathfinder and D&D 5e.
* [DiceRight](https://www.diceright.com/) - DiceRight is an online platform for playing tabletop games. It tracks game aspects like turns and damage to speed up gameplay and eliminate tedious elements.
* [DnD Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/) - :money_with_wings: :dragon: The official DnD character creation and management tool.
* [Improved initiative](https://www.improved-initiative.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/improvedinitiative) :dragon: A DnD 5e initiative tracker.
* [Kobold fight club](https://kobold.club/fight/#/encounter-builder) - :game_die: :dragon: [:unlock:](https://github.com/Asmor/5e-monsters) A DnD 5e encounter builder.
* [Paper forge](https://www.patreon.com/paperforge/posts) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/paperforge/posts) A subscription of token artwork for online play or printing.
* [Printable heroes](https://printableheroes.com/) - [![Patreon](./images/patreon.svg)](https://www.patreon.com/PrintableHeroes) A collection of filterable tokens that can be printed for tabletop encounters.
* [Ready? Set? Dice](https://readysetdice.com/) - Ready? Set? Dice! is a free to use combat tracker, party manager and encounter builder for the Pathfinder 2nd edition TTRPG system.
